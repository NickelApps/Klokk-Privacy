Privacy Policy for Klokk

Last updated: 25 July 2026

Klokk is a clock app — alarms, a world clock, a timer, and a stopwatch. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. The short version: Klokk does not collect, store, or share your personal data, and it has no accounts, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information Klokk does not collect

Klokk does not require an account and does not ask you to sign in. It does not collect your name, email address, contacts, device identifiers, or location from your device's GPS. It contains no advertising, no analytics or usage tracking, and no crash-reporting services. Nothing you do in the app is sent to us — in fact, there is no server of ours for it to be sent to.

Your alarms, folders, saved cities, timer presets, and settings are stored only on your device.

The one thing Klokk sends over the internet: weather

Klokk shows current weather for the cities in your world clock and on the alarm screen. To do this, it uses a third-party weather service, Open-Meteo. Two kinds of request are made, and only for weather:

  • Looking up a city. When you add a city, the city name you type is sent to Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find that city's coordinates and time zone.
  • Fetching weather. To show a city's weather, that city's coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo, which returns the current conditions.

These requests contain only what is needed to look up a place or its weather — a city name or a set of coordinates. They do not include your identity, your device's GPS location, or any account information, because Klokk has none of those. Open-Meteo's handling of requests is governed by its own terms and privacy practices, available at open-meteo.com/en/terms.

Children's privacy

Klokk is a general-purpose clock app and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Payments and support

Klokk is free. It includes an optional link to a third-party tip page for people who wish to support the app. Using it is entirely optional, and any payment is handled by that third-party service under its own terms — Klokk does not receive or store any payment information.
